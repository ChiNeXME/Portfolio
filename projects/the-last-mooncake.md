The Last Mooncake is a cozy game about a small bakery on the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival. Customers come in carrying stories, and the only way to bake them the right mooncake is to actually read those stories.

You play Artemis, a little cat who wanders into a bakery and finds an old, cracked mooncake mold. The Baker takes you in, and together you help customers rebuild mooncakes from their memories. Each time you get a recipe right, the moon gets a little brighter.

We made it for [Cozy Fall Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/cozy-fall-jam-2026) around the theme "Fall" and the optional theme "Roots".

## My part

I was the lead and a programmer on a team of four. I built the dialogue system that runs every conversation in the game: portraits swap in when each character speaks, their mouths move while they talk, and every character has their own voice. I also built the settings menu, the audio players for music and sound effects, and saving, and I did the UI and the portraits for the NPCs.
